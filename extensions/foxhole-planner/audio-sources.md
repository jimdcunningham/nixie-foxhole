# Black Bolt audio sources

The source recordings are available under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

| Bundled clip | Source | Excerpt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `steam-whistle.wav` | [Steam Whistle.mp3 by Bidone](https://freesound.org/people/Bidone/sounds/71778/) | 0.30–3.15 s, raised one semitone at the original duration |
| `steam-chuff.wav` | [single steam train chuff by birdOfTheNorth](https://freesound.org/people/birdOfTheNorth/sounds/623817/), extracted from [Keith Peter’s Hall-class locomotive field recording](https://freesound.org/people/keithpeter/sounds/125211/) | 0.015–0.470 s |
| `wheel-grind.wav` | [Metal grinding or drilling by cinningbao](https://freesound.org/people/cinningbao/sounds/365623/) | 9.80–14.40 s, slowed, filtered, and crossfaded for looping |
| `steam-idle.wav` | [d_s478_idlesteamhiss.wav by relwin](https://freesound.org/people/relwin/sounds/686057/), recorded on the Durango and Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad | 10.35–11.80 s |
| `steam-bell.wav` | [Bell #3 by Joseph Sardin](https://bigsoundbank.com/bell-3-s2115.html) | One strike from the MP3 preview, with 25 ms of lead-in trimmed and playback slowed to 24% |
| `steam-bell-loop.wav` | Same source and pitch as `steam-bell.wav` | First five seconds with a 350 ms tail fade. A new strike starts every 1.25 seconds while the bell is on. |
| `rail-rolling.wav` | Original synthesized audio, dedicated to the public domain under CC0 | Eight seconds of filtered noise for wheel contact and light track texture |
| `coupler-clank.wav` | [Metallic clunk.wav by michael_grinnell](https://freesound.org/people/michael_grinnell/sounds/512475/) | Original strike, filtered and faded |

The excerpts were edited and exported as mono 32 kHz, 16-bit PCM for positional playback. The idle clip uses the sustained part of the excerpt with a crossfaded loop seam. The chuff is pitched down three semitones.
