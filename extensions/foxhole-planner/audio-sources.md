# Black Bolt audio sources

All four recordings are available under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The bundled WAVs were edited from the public high-quality MP3 previews on the source pages.

| Bundled clip | Source | Excerpt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `steam-whistle.wav` | [Steam Whistle.mp3 by Bidone](https://freesound.org/people/Bidone/sounds/71778/) | 0.30–3.15 s |
| `steam-chuff.wav` | [single steam train chuff by birdOfTheNorth](https://freesound.org/people/birdOfTheNorth/sounds/623817/), extracted from [Keith Peter’s Hall-class locomotive field recording](https://freesound.org/people/keithpeter/sounds/125211/) | 0.015–0.470 s |
| `brake-squeal.wav` | [G47-16-Steam Train In Stop.wav by Craig Smith](https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/438765/), from the USC Cinema sound effects archive | 69.05–71.17 s |
| `steam-idle.wav` | [d_s478_idlesteamhiss.wav by relwin](https://freesound.org/people/relwin/sounds/686057/), recorded on the Durango and Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad | 10.35–11.80 s |

The excerpts were folded to mono, filtered, faded at the edit boundaries, and exported as 32 kHz, 16-bit PCM for positional playback.
